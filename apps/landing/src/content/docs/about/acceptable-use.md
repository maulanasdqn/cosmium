title: Acceptable use
description: The terms under which Cosmium may be used.
---

Cosmium is a browser automation and fingerprint-consistency tool. It is meant
for legitimate work: testing your own sites and applications, quality
assurance, research on browser fingerprinting, accessibility and compatibility
checks, and collecting publicly available data where you are permitted to.

## You agree to

- **Follow the law.** Comply with every law that applies to you and to the
  data you access, including computer-misuse, data-protection, privacy, and
  copyright law.
- **Respect the sites you visit.** Follow each website's terms of service and
  `robots.txt`, and stop when a site asks you to. Do not access a site, an
  account, or data you are not authorized to access.
- **Be a light load.** Rate-limit your requests so you never degrade a
  service for its other users.
- **Protect personal data.** Do not collect, store, or process personal
  information without a lawful basis, and handle any you do collect according
  to the applicable data-protection rules.

## You must not use Cosmium to

- gain unauthorized access to any system, account, or data;
- commit fraud, impersonate others, or create fake accounts at scale;
- take over accounts, stuff credentials, or test stolen credentials;
- abuse ticketing, checkout, voting, or promotion systems;
- send spam or carry out any attack against a service, including
  denial-of-service;
- infringe intellectual-property rights or scrape content you have no right to
  reproduce;
- break any law or any agreement that binds you.

If you are unsure whether a use is permitted, ask the site owner or a lawyer
before you start.
